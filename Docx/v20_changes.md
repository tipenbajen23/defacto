# v19 -> v20: what changed in the reviewer

Edits applied in place, in the document's own format and styles. No markers of any
kind were added to the document itself.

## Digests corrected
- Bernardo v. NLRC — Facts, ISSUE, RULING, [LEGAL BASIS], [APPLICATION], [CONCLUSION]:
  27 of the 43 complainants (those whose renewals ran past six months) are regular.
- Buenaflor v. Stolt-Nielsen — Facts and [APPLICATION]: sign-on 25 July, inspection two
  days later, dismissal 22 August; the ruling rests on failure of proof, not on a
  "single incident" rule.
- Paz v. Northern Tobacco — [CONCLUSION]: backwages, nominal damages for want of due
  process, retirement pay under Art. 302, financial assistance.
- PT&T v. NLRC — [CONCLUSION]: reinstatement with backwages reduced by the three-month
  suspension upheld for the concealment. Dropped the uncitable Zialcita cross-reference.
- Padilla v. Airborne — [CONCLUSION]: separation pay, not reinstatement.
- Salenga v. CA — [CONCLUSION]: partial grant, Colayco released, remand for computation
  under R.A. 8282.
- Millares and Lagda — [CONCLUSION]: 100% of the CEIP credited contributions.
- Minsola v. New City Builders — [CONCLUSION]: the differentials, holiday pay and fees
  actually awarded.
- Grand Asian Shipping v. Galvez — Facts and [CONCLUSION]: the pilferage, the split
  dispositions on the dismissals, the captain's unpaid salaries.
- People v. Urquico — RULING: "positive and categorical"; added the malum prohibitum /
  malum in se ground.
- Santiago v. CF Sharp — new six-part digest supplied (perfection vs commencement;
  US$4,635 for nine months plus 10% fees). The accreditation rules under it are now
  headed as DMW/POEA rules, and the solidary-liability note now cites Questcore.
- Century Properties v. Babiano — new six-part digest supplied, with a line
  distinguishing Portillo.
- BMG Records v. Aparecio — RULING expanded: the employer discharged the burden there.

## Doctrinal statements corrected
- Pars. 14 and 26 — the two-or-more-persons proviso is a rule of evidence, not an element.
- Casual employment — the duration "element" removed; residual definition added.
- Night workers — Art. 154's seven hours including midnight to 5 a.m., distinguished from
  Art. 86's 10 p.m. to 6 a.m.
- Company practice — first requisite reversed to "voluntarily given, not by reason of a
  legal or contractual obligation"; burden on the employee added.
- BFOQ — "all or substantially all" restored; Yrasuegui's separation pay added.
- Non-compete — "reasonable as to time, trade and place" added as a requisite.
- Two-tiered test (par. 1444) — the two tiers are the four-fold test and economic
  dependence; the economic tier supplements rather than displaces.
- Felicilda — decided on the four-fold test and control.
- Jose Rizal College — hourly-paid faculty exempt for regular holidays in all vacations;
  entitled on special holidays and when classes are called off.
- Officers' solidary liability (par. 447) — not automatic.
- Art. 136 "substantial period" — as determined by the Secretary, not fixed at six months.

## Added
- Condonation, express and implied, with the clean slate rule.
- Past violation + subsequent similar violation (incorrigibility).
- Limitations on the power to discipline.
- Par. 3241's formula read down to match the warning analysis.

## Tables

  stated at par. 350.
- Table 10: Sonza and Tiangco figures separated and put on their stated bases.

## Not changed
