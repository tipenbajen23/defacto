## The repeal, verified

R.A. No. 12063, Sec. 36 repeals R.A. No. 7686 (Dual Training System Act of 1994),
**Book Two, Title II, Chapters 1 and 2 of P.D. No. 442**, and Sec. 34(A)(1)(a)(v)
of the NIRC. Against the Labor Code's own structure:

| Chapter | Articles | Subject | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chapter I | Arts. 57–72 | Apprentices | **Repealed** |
| Chapter II | Arts. 73–77 | Learners | **Repealed** |
| Chapter III | Arts. 78–81 | Handicapped Workers | **In force** |

So the repeal is exactly Arts. 57 to 77, and Arts. 78 to 81 survive. The IRR says
the same thing in Rule XIII, Sec. 5.

## One discrepancy, surfaced rather than silently resolved

The Act, Sec. 4(a), defines **Apprenticeship** as covering "higher levels of
competencies covered by **National Certificates (NC) 3 and above**". The IRR's own
definition section (Rule I, Sec. 6[b]) says "**including but not limited to**, programs
covered by National Certificates (NC) **III and above**", while its program-specific
guideline (Rule V, Sec. 2[a]) says "**NC II and above**". The reviewer now states the
Act's NC 3 threshold, notes both IRR formulations, and says the Act prevails over the
IRR — rather than picking one and hiding the conflict.
